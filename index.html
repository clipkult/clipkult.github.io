About ClipKult

ClipKult posts the best short moments from German Twitch streams (GTA 6 and more) as vertical clips on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram and Facebook.

The clips are made with "peak cut", our own desktop app. It records public streams, finds the strongest moments and renders short clips with captions. It posts only to ClipKult's own accounts.
