Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

ClipKult publishes short highlight clips from public Twitch streams on TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. To do this we use a private desktop app called "peak cut", which runs only on our own computer. This policy explains what data the app handles when it connects to TikTok.

Who uses the app

peak cut is used only by the ClipKult team to manage the ClipKult accounts. It is not offered to the public, and other people cannot sign in to it.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information of the connected TikTok account (display name, avatar and an account identifier), so the app can show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens that let the app upload videos to that account.
  • The status of uploaded videos (for example "processing" or "published").

We do not access followers, messages, comments, likes or any content of other TikTok users.

How we use it

The data is used only to upload ClipKult's own clips, with their titles and hashtags, to ClipKult's own TikTok account, and to show whether an upload worked.

Storage and security

Tokens and profile information are stored only on the ClipKult computer that runs the app. They are not sent to any other server, sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting data

Disconnecting the TikTok account in the app deletes its tokens and profile information. Access can also be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services.
