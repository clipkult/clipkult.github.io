Terms of Service

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The service

ClipKult Studio is a private desktop tool that records public Twitch streams, cuts short highlight clips and posts them to ClipKult's own social media accounts. It is used only by the ClipKult team.

Use of TikTok

The app connects to TikTok only through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, and only with the account owner's permission. It follows TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Developer Terms. Posts are spaced out over time and are never sent to accounts other than ClipKult's own.

Content

Clips are short excerpts of public streams with added captions and commentary. If you are a streamer and want a clip removed, contact us and we will delete it.

No warranty

The app is provided as is, without any warranty. We are not liable for interruptions of the platforms it connects to.
